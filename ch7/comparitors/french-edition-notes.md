# French-edition comparison notes (Roy)

A working record of places where the French text in `comparitors/roy.md` is worth comparing with the first-edition German in `ch7_de.md`. This is a comparison aid, not a claim that every difference is an editorial change by Marx: a French divergence may reflect Roy's translation, Marx's revision, or both. Roy and Fowkes are witnesses for comparison only, not authorities for interpreting the philosophical categories. Their renderings can obscure or flatten the conceptual distinctions tracked in the German; the English translation must therefore be made from the first-edition German under the supplied glossary and Fineschi's method. The present notes are intentionally limited to examples from the opening of the chapter; expand them against the complete texts before drawing broader conclusions.

| Location | First-edition German | Roy French | Comparison note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter title | *Arbeitsprozess und Verwerthungsprozess* | *Production de valeurs d’usage et production de la plus-value* | The French heading substitutes a pair of product/value categories for the German process-pair. This is a substantial reframing, not a literal rendering of the heading. |
| First paragraph: labor made into commodities | “Um seine Arbeit in Waaren darzustellen, muss er sie vor allem in Gebrauchswerthen darstellen” | “Pour que celui-ci produise des marchandises, son travail doit être utile, c’est-à-dire se réaliser en valeurs d’usage.” | German explicitly says to present/embody the labor in commodities and first of all in use-values; Roy recasts this as labor having to be useful and actualize itself in use-values. Note `darstellen` and `realisieren` carefully against the glossary. |
| First paragraph: capitalist's control | “für den Kapitalisten und unter seiner Kontrole” | “pour le compte du capitaliste et sous sa direction” | `für` is represented as “on behalf of”; `Kontrole` as “direction.” The French makes the supervisory relation more explicitly directional. |
| First paragraph: social form | “unabhängig von jeder bestimmten gesellschaftlichen Form” | “abstraction faite de tout cachet particulier que peut lui imprimer telle ou telle phase du progrès économique de la société” | Roy expands “any determinate social form” into a particular stamp imparted by a phase of society's economic progress. The expansion adds a developmental gloss absent from the German sentence. |
| Opening definition of the labor process | “durch seine eigne That vermittelt, regelt und kontrolirt” | “par son action, il joue ... le rôle d’une puissance naturelle” / “il agit par ce mouvement” | Roy reorganizes the syntax; the German's sequence “mediates, regulates, controls” the metabolism is not reproduced in the corresponding opening sentence. |
| Purposeful work | “zweckmässige Thätigkeit” | “activité personnelle de l'homme” | Roy renders the purposeful/ends-directed determination as “personal activity”; this loses the explicit `Zweck` connection. |
| Object of labor | “Arbeitsgegenstand” | “objet de travail” | A fairly direct lexical match, useful to distinguish from the German `Gegenstand` used more generally. |
| Instrument of labor | “Arbeitsmittel” | “moyen de travail” | Direct lexical correspondence; preserve as a distinct technical term from `Produktionsmittel` / “moyen de production.” |

## Conventions for continued annotation

- Quote short German and French phrases and give paragraph location, rather than silently treating French as a second German witness.
- Distinguish (a) a changed heading or division, (b) a translation choice, (c) a genuine textual addition/omission, and (d) a wording known to have been revised by Marx. Do not infer (d) from a difference alone.
- When possible, record the exact French witness/printing and compare the French passage against the relevant German edition or manuscript evidence before labeling a change “Marx's.”
- Keep these notes separate from the English translation. The translation should follow the first-edition German paragraph by paragraph; Roy and Fowkes are comparison witnesses only.
